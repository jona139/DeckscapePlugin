# RuneLite XP reward economy expectations

These are long-run averages for eligible XP earned on normal main-game worlds. Pack and currency rewards use independent server-authoritative XP tracks. The figures exclude Stardust from opening packs, challenges, website play, promotions, and other reward sources.

## Independent reward tracks

### Pack track

- One pack roll per **50,000 eligible XP**.
- **50%** chance to receive one core pack; **50%** chance for no pack.
- Awarded core packs are General, Combat, or Skilling with equal probability.
- Faction packs, including Misthalin packs, cannot roll from XP.

This averages **0.5 core packs per 50,000 XP**, or one pack per 100,000 XP. Each individual core-pack type averages one per 300,000 XP.

### Currency track

- One guaranteed currency outcome per **10,000 eligible XP**.
- **80%** chance to receive **100–200 Coins**, averaging 150 on a coin outcome.
- **20%** chance to receive **1–3 Stardust**, averaging 2 on a Stardust outcome.

Each currency roll therefore averages **120 Coins and 0.4 Stardust**. That is 1,200 Coins and 4 Stardust per 100,000 eligible XP. Short sessions can vary substantially.

## Expected hourly income

Activity descriptions are illustrative; XP/hour determines the expectation.

| Eligible XP/hour | Example pace | Pack rolls/hour | Core packs/hour | Each core pack/hour | Currency rolls/hour | Coins/hour | Stardust/hour |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 25,000 | Low-intensity activity | 0.50 | 0.25 | 0.08 | 2.50 | 300 | 1 |
| 50,000 | Moderate activity | 1.00 | 0.50 | 0.17 | 5.00 | 600 | 2 |
| 100,000 | Efficient skilling/combat | 2.00 | 1.00 | 0.33 | 10.00 | 1,200 | 4 |
| 200,000 | Fast training method | 4.00 | 2.00 | 0.67 | 20.00 | 2,400 | 8 |
| 400,000 | High-intensity method | 8.00 | 4.00 | 1.33 | 40.00 | 4,800 | 16 |
| 800,000 | Extreme/burst XP method | 16.00 | 8.00 | 2.67 | 80.00 | 9,600 | 32 |

Formulae for any other eligible activity rate `X`:

- pack rolls/hour = `X / 50,000`
- packs/hour = `X / 50,000 × 0.50`
- currency rolls/hour = `X / 10,000`
- Coins/hour = `X / 10,000 × 0.80 × 150`
- Stardust/hour = `X / 10,000 × 0.20 × 2`

Leagues, Deadman, Last Man Standing, PvP/high-risk worlds, PvP Arena, beta, tournament, speedrunning, no-save, and other excluded world types contribute no eligible XP.
